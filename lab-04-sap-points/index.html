    Sap Points

    Idea

    What if skill points were sap? A skill tree taken literally. You don't spend points in a menu: you play, and every move pumps sap from the roots up into its branch. Buds swell and bloom into skills, and the branch grows on to the next one. Pruning is the respec: cut a branch and every drop runs back down.

    Controls

    • Hold Strike, Cast or Sneak (or keys 1, 2, 3) to feed a branch.
    • Tap a bud or a flower to read the skill.
    • Swipe across a branch to prune it. The sap goes back to the roots.
    • No swipe? Open a skill and press "Prune from here".

    Inspired by

    • An "always-on" agent diagram seen on X: a system you can watch working while data flows along its connections. Only that principle was taken.
    • Skyrim's perk constellations: a skill tree drawn as the thing it is named after.
    • Hades' Mirror of Night: a free respec with a full refund invites players to experiment.

    For game UI

    Most skill trees hide the link between how you play and what you unlock. Let usage feed progress, show the points travelling to where they land, and lock nothing in for good: a respec that is a visible, reversible gesture with a full refund turns the tree into a place to experiment.
