Train of Thought

Idea

What if a dialogue choice was a train of thought, literally? Your answer is a little train that leaves the question and picks up words at every stop. The switches decide which words it collects, so you can read where each track leads before you commit. Flip a switch while the train is on it and you lose your train of thought.

Controls

  • Each fork has a glowing ring marked SWITCH. Its arrow points to the branch the train will take. Tap the ring to send the train the other way. The dashed line shows the whole route ahead.
  • Think starts the train and the camera rides along behind it. Each word it reaches hops onto a new carriage, and the engine puffs out letters.
  • You can still change a switch the train hasn't reached yet. Flip one while the train is crossing it and it derails.
  • At the end of the line the carriages unload into your reply, and the guard answers.
  • Keyboard: 1, 2 and 3 flip the switches, Space thinks, R resets, C toggles the follow camera.

Inspired by

  • ama_plaka's circuit-board GIFs, where a signal visibly runs along the traces and something grows out of the board.
  • Branching dialogue wheels and trees in narrative RPGs, where the reply is chosen before it is spoken.
  • Model railway layouts, the switch levers of a signal box, and chase cameras in racing and train games.

For game UI

Show the path, not just the options. When players can see where each choice leads, and see the reply assemble as it travels, a dialogue pick reads as a decision with momentum instead of a list of labels. The same pattern works for crafting chains, quest routes or any branching confirm: a visible route ahead, a point of no return you can feel, and a clear payoff at the end of the line.
