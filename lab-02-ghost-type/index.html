Ghost Type

Idea

What if a ghost was made of the words it can't stop thinking? A pun on "ghost type": a spirit set in type, whose mood rewrites the letters it is made of. Rush in and it scrambles. Stay still and it calms down. Leave after it trusted you and it remembers.

Controls

  • Move the cursor or drag a finger toward the ghost. Fast movement spooks it.
  • Hold still over it for a second to calm it. Keep still to earn its trust.
  • Move away (or lift your finger) once it trusts you.
  • Click or tap it to say boo. Keyboard: arrows move, Space startles, Esc leaves.

Inspired by

  • @_1mposter: silhouettes filled with repeated words and ASCII glyphs, neon on black, where the text gives the image its meaning.
  • Pac-Man's ghosts: a tiny AI state machine (chase, scatter, frightened) you read instantly through colour and behaviour.
  • Concrete poetry and Apollinaire's calligrams, where the shape of the text is part of what it says.

For game UI

Companion affinity, enemy alert levels and NPC trust usually hide in a meter. Let the state change the material instead: the same object, rewritten in a new word, colour and motion. The mood row underneath doubles as a readable debug view of the AI, which is handy in playtests.
