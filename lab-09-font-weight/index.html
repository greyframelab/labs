Font Weight

Idea

What if your word had to float? The letters you type become a raft, lashed together with rope. Font weight is literal here: bolder letters have more area, so they float higher and carry more cargo. Each letter's real shape decides where the raft is buoyant and where it tips. Load it until something gives.

Controls

  • Type a word (up to 10 letters). The raft rebuilds with the cargo still on board.
  • Weight goes from Thin to Black. Thin a loaded raft and watch it sink.
  • Click or tap the sky to drop the next cargo right there, or press Drop. Arrow keys move the hook and Enter drops.
  • Swell adds waves. Off-centre loads tilt the raft, and past a point cargo slides overboard.
  • The white dashed line painted on the letters is the load line. Water above it means trouble.

Inspired by

  • ことば橋 by @yorimichi: a typed word becomes a bridge, tested by heavier and heavier vehicles.
  • The Plimsoll line, the load mark painted on ship hulls since the 1870s to show how deep a ship may safely sit.
  • Scribblenauts, where the words you write become objects with real behaviour.

For game UI

When players type something (a ship name, a guild banner, a spell), let it carry real stats and show the result in the world. Put the limit in the scene too, like a load line on the hull, so players read the risk before they fail. And make failure readable and funny, so testing the limit is the fun part.
