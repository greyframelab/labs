Pulling Strings

Idea

What if the controls were the puppet's strings? Pull a thread and the skeleton lifts that bone. Let go and the thread rings like a string. Strum across them all and he dances, and if you keep the rattle going the stage turns into a danse macabre.

Controls

  • Drag a thread to lift that limb. Let go to pluck it: the further you pulled, the louder it rings.
  • Sweep across the threads to strum. On touch, press on the empty stage and sweep.
  • Drag the wooden control to walk him across the stage. Grab a bone to throw him around.
  • Keyboard: 1–5 pluck a thread, arrows move the control, Space strums.

Inspired by

  • A piece by dmvrg shared by the Three.js account on X, where fine lines answer a touch like taut strings.
  • The marionette control: a wooden cross where every thread is one limb, so the puppeteer's hand is the whole interface.
  • Karplus–Strong string synthesis (1983): each note is a burst of noise ringing in a short delay line.

For game UI

Put the input on stage. When the controls are physically tied to the character, every press reads as cause and effect without a tutorial. Feedback lives where the player is looking (the thread, the bone, the shadow), and a combo meter that changes the whole scene rewards rhythm better than a number in a corner.
