Turntable

Idea

What if a level's layout was its soundtrack? Each ring of this tower holds one layer of the song. Turn a ring and its part slides in time against the others, so the music rearranges itself while you play the puzzle. When every rune sits over the door, the groove locks and the way opens.

Controls

  • Drag a ring left or right to turn it. Flick it to spin.
  • Keyboard: up and down pick a ring, left and right turn it one step.
  • Click a layer in the director panel to select its ring.
  • Scramble mixes the tower again. Demo solves it for you.

How it works

Each ring is a 32-step loop wrapped around the tower. The light that sweeps around it is the playhead. The song is a two-step garage loop; when every ring fits, the bass drops into a half-time wobble. Turning a ring shifts its loop against the grid, so the bass can land under the wrong chord or the kick on the wrong beat. Only one position per ring fits. All the sound is synthesised live in the browser.

Inspired by

  • "Remixing with a Rubik's cube" by be.of.dao on Instagram, where every turn of a cube remixes a loop.
  • Adaptive music in games, where layers fade in and out with what the player does.

For game UI

Puzzles feel better when you can hear that you are getting closer. Give each part of the solution its own voice, show which part is right in a panel the player can read at a glance, and save one big moment for when it all clicks.
