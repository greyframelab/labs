Jump Start

Idea

What if a crafting screen had to be jump-started? Bolt scrap together, give it a heart, and throw the switch. Power surges from part to part, the machine wakes up, and rings around it tell you how good a build it is.

Controls

  • Drag parts from the tray onto the bench, or press one to add it automatically. Parts snap to whatever they touch.
  • Drag a part back to the tray to scrap it.
  • Jump start powers the machine. Parts only work if they are bolted, directly or through others, to a heart.
  • If the parts need more power than the hearts and coils give, the machine glitches. Add a coil or remove something.

How it works

Touching parts form a graph. The power surge travels through it outward from the heart, so you can see the order things wake up in. Cables are simulated ropes strung between neighbours. The glitch is not decoration: its strength is the power shortfall, so a stable build looks clean.

Inspired by

  • Two maximalist scrap-machine collages seen on Instagram (author unknown).
  • Build-and-test loops like the vehicle workshops in Banjo-Kazooie: Nuts & Bolts and Zelda: Tears of the Kingdom.
  • Loot rarity tiers, where colour and glow carry the value.

For game UI

Crafting feels better when the result is felt, not just listed. Show the order systems come online, make failure readable (here, glitch equals power shortfall), and give the reward a moment of its own instead of a number in a corner.
