Time Loaf

Idea

What if a jump was a loaf of time? One platformer double jump is baked into a single solid: every frame of the move is stacked behind the one before, so the whole action becomes an object you can walk around. The hero is drawn as rings of points, and each ring traces a tube through time. Drag the knife along the timeline to slice the loaf and see the exact instant, or switch to Moment to watch the jump play the normal way.

Controls

  • Drag the scene to orbit. Scroll or pinch to zoom.
  • Drag the blue knife on the timeline to slice through time. The part already sliced is solid, the rest stays as a pale ghost.
  • Loaf stacks every frame in depth. Moment collapses it back to a single figure.
  • Space plays or pauses, the arrow keys step one frame, L switches the view, R resets the camera.

Inspired by

  • "Battle of points" by @aicreataro on X, an action scene turned into a point cloud. Only the principle of freezing an action as points to study it was taken.
  • Étienne-Jules Marey's chronophotography (1880s), which recorded one movement as a run of positions on a single plate.
  • Umberto Boccioni's sculpture "Unique Forms of Continuity in Space" (1913), a body in motion shown as one solid form.

For game UI

A jump is judged by its arc, its hang time and how readable the landing is, and none of that fits in a single frame. A space-time view turns the whole move into one shape you can read at a glance: where it clears the gap, where it hangs, where the landing arrives late. It works for replay scrubbers, photo and kill-cam modes, and in-editor tools for animators and level designers.
