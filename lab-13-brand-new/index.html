Your guild name, forged into a branding iron. Heat it, then burn it in.

Lab #13 · UI · 17 Oct 2026

Brand New

Idea

What if your guild name was forged into a branding iron? Every letter you type welds one more ring onto the iron, and the first letter sets how many times the pattern repeats. The same name always forges the same iron. Then you heat it and burn your mark into a plank: press too early and the mark comes out faint, wait too long and it scorches.

Controls

  • Type in "Brand name" (letters, numbers and spaces, up to 14). Each letter welds a new ring onto the iron: arcs, spokes, chevrons, studs, leaves or spikes. A space adds a plain border.
  • Hold Heat, hold anywhere on the scene, or hold Space to heat the iron. The gauge follows a blacksmith's heat colours, from black heat to white heat.
  • Brand, a quick tap on the scene, or Enter presses the iron into the wood. Press while the needle is in the gold band for a perfect mark.
  • New plank brings fresh wood. Random name forges another guild's iron. Sound turns the forge sounds on or off.

Inspired by

  • SIGILIZERSIXHUNDRED by niftymonkee: a word that turns into a sigil, letter by letter.
  • Blacksmith heat-colour charts, which read the temperature of steel from its glow, from dull red to white heat.
  • Leather and livestock branding irons: one welded symbol, pressed hot, readable for decades.

For game UI

Crest editors usually hand players a grid of icons and colours. Here the name the player already picked becomes the art, so every guild gets a unique emblem that any client can rebuild from the text, with no image to store or sync. Turning the reveal into a short skill check (heat, wait, press) makes creating the emblem a moment players remember and share, and the mark quality gives them a reason to try again. The same pattern fits weapon engravings, ship liveries, ranch brands in a farming sim or the wax seal on a quest letter.
