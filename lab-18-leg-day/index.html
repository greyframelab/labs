Leg Day

Idea

What if you could build a creature and it learned to walk on the spot? Add legs, stretch the bones, shift the weight, and the gait works itself out. Nothing is keyframed: every step is decided live.

Controls

  • Move the pointer, or tap and drag, to set where it walks.
  • Use the rig panel to add or remove leg pairs, change bone length and weight.
  • Walk, Wave and Trot change which feet may lift at the same time.
  • Ghosts leaves a trail of past poses. Play demo runs a short choreography.

How it works

Each leg is a two-bone chain solved with inverse kinematics. A foot stays locked to the floor until it drifts too far from where the hip wants it, then it lifts, arcs to a predicted landing spot and plants. The gait is only a rule about which neighbours can be in the air together. The slip readout stays at zero because a planted foot never moves.

Inspired by

  • A procedural creature Reel by @rybinfx on Instagram.
  • Rain World's procedurally animated creatures.
  • Spore's creature editor, where any body you build can walk.

For game UI

Character and mount editors feel alive when every change shows up in motion straight away. Let players see how a choice changes the way something moves, not just how it looks, and give them a readout that proves it (here, foot slip).
