Elbow Room

A backpack whose pockets shove over to fit what you find.

Lab #10 · UI · 13 Oct 2026

Elbow Room

Idea

What if inventory slots made room for each other? Every pocket in this pack is a living cell. Pack something and the cells shove over to fit it: bulky gear muscles in, small things get small pockets, and the free space gathers in the gaps. Push past the limit and a seam pops.

Controls

  • Tap anything under Found nearby to pack it. It takes the biggest free pocket and elbows the rest aside.
  • Drag a packed item to move it. The other pockets flow around it.
  • Tap a packed item, or drag it out of the pack, to unpack it.
  • The load bar shows litres used against the pack's 24 L. Overpack it and every pocket feels the squeeze. Go past about 29 L and the last thing you added bursts out.
  • Shake repacks everything from scratch. Fixed slots puts the same gear in twelve equal pockets, for comparison.
  • Keyboard: Tab to the pack, arrow keys pick an item, Enter unpacks it, S shakes.

Inspired by

  • Joanie Lemercier's generative cell networks, which grow around empty space like something alive.
  • Voronoi treemaps (Balzer & Deussen, 2005), where each cell's area is tuned to a value.
  • Grid inventories in survival and action games, where packing is a puzzle of fixed squares.

For game UI

Let inventory space read like space. When slots size themselves to what they hold, players see bulk at a glance, free room shows up as real gaps, and "full" is something you feel coming instead of a sudden error. Keep the grid when packing is the puzzle. Use living pockets when it isn't.
