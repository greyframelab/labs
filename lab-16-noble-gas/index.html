Noble Gas

Idea

What if a sign designer worked with real gas? In neon signs the colour comes from the gas in the tube: neon burns red-orange, argon blue, helium pink. Write your sign, fill each line with a noble gas and watch it strike, spill into the fog and shine on the wet road.

Controls

  • Type in Text to change the selected line. Tap a line on the sign to select it.
  • Pick a gas tile to refill that line's tubes.
  • Block or Script changes the lettering.
  • Wear makes old tubes sputter and fail. Fog and Rain change how the light travels.
  • Power switches the sign off and strikes it on again. Hum adds the transformer buzz.

Inspired by

  • Dystopika by Zoadle: a cyberpunk city builder with giant billboards and holograms in the fog.
  • Real neon: each gas glows its own colour, and tubes are bent and filled one letter at a time.
  • Roadside diner and motel signs, where one dead letter becomes part of the place.

For game UI

Customisation screens usually show a flat swatch. Show the item where it will live, in the world's light, weather and wear, and players choose with confidence. It fits shop signs, guild banners, ship liveries and base decorations.
